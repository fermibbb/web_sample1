Our Story

Clay from the hills, kilned by hand.

Dar Safi was founded by Master Mohamed El-Khattabi in 1958, in a small workshop overlooking the Atlantic.

We dig our clay from the red hills outside Safi, the same earth our grandfathers used. Every bowl, tagine, and vase is shaped on a foot-driven wheel, painted free-hand with natural pigments, and fired in wood-burning kilns.

No two pieces are identical. Each one carries the fingerprints of the artisan who made it — a quiet signature passed down through generations.

Our Craft

From earth to vessel.

— Step 01

Shaping

Red clay from the Safi hills is wedged, centered on a foot-driven wheel, and pulled into form by our master throwers.

— Step 02

Painting

Each piece is hand-painted free-form with natural mineral pigments — no stencils, no transfers, just inherited patterns.

— Step 03

Firing

Finished pieces are kilned twice in wood-burning ovens, sealing the glaze and locking in the deep, layered colors.
